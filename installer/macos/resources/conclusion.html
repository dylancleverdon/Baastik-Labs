Norg is installed

Open (or restart) your DAW and look for Norg by Baastik Labs in your instrument plug-ins.

  • Logic Pro: Instrument slot → AU Instruments → Baastik Labs → Norg
  • Ableton Live: Plug-Ins → VST3 → Baastik Labs → Norg
  • Standalone: the Norg app in your Applications folder (in your home folder)

Updates install automatically. You can turn this off or roll back from Norg's System button.
