Text To Synth by Baastik Labs

Describe a sound to Claude, get a Serum 2 preset. Then shape it in plain English: "drier", "too harsh", "more movement".

This installs Text To Synth and connects it to Claude Desktop (and Claude Code, if you use it). You need Serum 2 and a Claude account.

If Claude is open, you'll restart it at the end.
