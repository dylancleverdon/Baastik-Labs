# Pickless

An Android app for an elimination diet: **become less picky, safely.**

- **Check a food**: scan a barcode, type a food or dish, or photograph an ingredients label. You get
  a verdict against your avoid list (with the exact label words that triggered it, including hidden
  names like whey, casein and soy lecithin) and a FODMAP score per serving with a portion slider.
- **Log** what you eat in two taps, BMs on the Bristol scale (1-7, no photos), symptoms, and a
  20-second evening check-in (sleep, stress, nicotine, drinks, meds).
- **Trigger detective**: compares what came before your bad BMs with everything else, from your first
  log, with an "early read" banner until the data is solid. "Why was that one bad?" lists the likely
  culprits for any BM.
- **Coach**: guided reintroduction tests, one food at a time (dairy is tested as lactose-free milk,
  then hard cheese, then regular milk, so lactose and dairy protein are told apart), which update
  your avoid list and personal FODMAP score.
- **Reports** for your doctor or dietitian (PDF), CSV export, backups, a restaurant card, shopping
  mode, a home-screen widget, optional Health Connect, an auto-updater and a suggestion box.

Everything stays on the phone. Setup: [docs/SETUP.md](docs/SETUP.md). Feature list: [docs/SPEC.md](docs/SPEC.md).

FODMAP numbers are estimates compiled from published research and dietitian guidance; product data
comes from Open Food Facts (ODbL) and USDA FoodData Central (public domain). Pickless gives
suspects and estimates, not medical advice.
